---
title: How to Contribute
description: How to contribute to Caracal, including ways to show support, report bugs, and more.
---

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Caracal. Your involvement is greatly appreciated and all contributions are welcome. Here are some ways you can help improve this project:

1. Show your support by giving the project a star on [GitHub](https://github.com/leanhtuan1994/react-native-caracal). This helps increase visibility and attract more contributors.

2. Share your thoughts and ideas by [opening an issue](https://github.com/leanhtuan1994/react-native-caracal/issues). If you have suggestions or feedback about any aspect of the project, we are always eager to hear from you.

3. If you have questions about the project, please open an [issue](https://github.com/leanhtuan1994/react-native-caracal/issues/new) with enough context for us to provide a helpful response.

4. If you encounter a bug or typo while using the starter kit or reading the documentation, we would be grateful if you could bring it to our attention. You can open an issue to report it, or even better, submit a pull request with a fix.

We value the input and contributions of our community and look forward to working with you to improve this project.
